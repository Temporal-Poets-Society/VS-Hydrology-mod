# Hydrology — water that fills, steps down and becomes rapids (Vintage Story 1.22)

Hydrology was built to provide water mechanics for the rivers generated by [Earth](https://mods.vintagestory.at/earth)
and add channel filling, smoother drops, rapids and working mill races. Earth supplies river geography; Hydrology
handles the water. This release is intended for Earth worlds. Standalone gameplay outside Earth has not been tested
for this release; historical synthetic lab controls do not establish standalone compatibility.

Current version: **0.6.2-rc.8**, the release candidate for Earth 2.0 (calm rivers with Earth's river query version 3;
[release notes](docs/release-0.6.2.md), [decision 0004](docs/decisions/0004-release-0.6.2.md)). What changed in each
version is in the [changelog](CHANGELOG.md).

## What it does

| Rule | In the game |
|---|---|
| **H1** Supported sources stay put | Falling water does not replace a supported pool source with falling water. Unsupported sources can drain under the settling rules. |
| **H2** Filling | A 2-wide channel dug from a river, lake or the sea fills with source water to its end, opened last or dug outward, fresh or salt. A 1-wide channel still carries 6 blocks of flowing water, unless vessels are enabled and water encloses it. With vessels enabled, uneven beds also fill from wide source sheets through continuous water up to 15 blocks above the supported cell: salt water, or fresh water outside Earth's natural river columns. Natural river steps keep their levels. With vessels enabled, an enclosed pocket fills too: a basin dug into a river's edge where it overlaps the river, or a 1-wide nook or ditch enclosed by water. Water fills only where its level is held: the space at and below it must close against walls and water, so the sea, open lakes and open land never rise. A space fills only from water that could fill it: the water bounding it, from the space's floor up, must be at least half the space, unless it is a natural river, so a canal dug from a lake, a pool, the sea or a river fills. A pond a player opens (a rim or bank broken) loses its water above the opening: the water runs downhill and collects where it comes to rest, the same amount; the sea, rivers and large lakes (4,096 sources or more) take it without rising; with no lower land within 8 blocks of the opening the pond keeps it. |
| **H3** 1-block drops are flattened | Water stepping down one block onto lower water settles into a ramp of flowing water (heights 6 to 1) on the lower pool, with no vertical face. Water in the air is falling water: no column of still water stands in a waterfall. |
| **H4** A fall of 2 or more makes rapids | Where the water surface falls 2 or more blocks within a few blocks of a source's edge, the edge becomes a rapid-water source. That is a single drop of 2 or more (any height), or a chute of 1-block steps up to 3 blocks apart, as Earth draws rapids of up to 4 blocks. Below it run rapid water: falls, and rapid ramps on each step. Fill the fall back to less than 2 and the edges become ordinary source water again (where Earth's river query marks rapids, the water stays rapid). A lone 1-block step stays an ordinary ramp (H3). Vanilla water wheels turn in such water: a mill race needs only a 2-block drop. |
| **H5** Local and cheap | It hooks vanilla's own liquid updates (a neighbour change schedules a delayed update). Searches are bounded, still water is never ticked, and the server decides everything; clients see ordinary block changes. |
| **Buckets: vanilla setting** | Strongly recommended: enable vanilla's "Prevent liquid source transport by buckets" under Survival challenges. Hydrology neither declares a default nor blocks source transport; the world or server setting decides. This replaces the old H6 default override. |

Only fresh water makes rapids: vanilla has no salt rapid water. A lone rapid source with no source beside it (a vanilla
rivulet spring) is left as it is.

## Compatibility

- **Not compatible with Realistic Water or Realistic Rapids.** Both replace the same spreading behaviour. Hydrology logs
  a warning at start-up if either is installed. Their ideas were studied for Hydrology; they have no license, and none
  of their code is used.
- Vanilla blocks only: removing Hydrology leaves no unknown blocks.
- Server-side water rules. Since 0.6.1 the mod is required on clients too (Earth is client-required and depends on it);
  on the client it only reduces rapid-water foam and calm-river specks. Use matching releases with Earth: Earth 2.0
  needs Hydrology 0.6.2 with river query version 3, so every new Earth world gets calm rivers (pool layout 11).
  Standalone gameplay is outside this release's tested scope.
- Game updates: every Harmony patch and every reflection on the game's non-public members goes through one registry
  (`src/GamePatches.cs`). If a game update removes or changes a target, Hydrology logs one warning naming the feature and
  switches off only that feature (the water rules as a whole, a refinement of them, the wheels, the foam, or the
  chunk-pack fix); it never stops the server.

## Configuration

`ModConfig/hydrology.json`:

| Key | Default | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `Fill` | true | H2 |
| `FillVessels` | false | Legacy fallback: enable communicating vessels directly |
| `WorldMayFillVessels` | true | Legacy fallback: allow Earth's saved vessel flags to enable vessels |
| `VesselsDepth`, `VesselsContained` | true | H2's vessels case fills only from water as deep as the water under the cell, and only a cell with no dry ledge beside it |
| `FillPockets` | true | H2 (0.6.2, with the vessels case on): enclosed pockets fill, in natural river water too |
| `RefillOnSource` | 3 | H2: a flowing cell on a source with this many source sides becomes a source (0: off) |
| `SupplyFactor` | 2 | H2: a held space fills only when the water bounding it, from the space's floor up, is at least 1/SupplyFactor of the space, or stands in a natural river column (0: off; decision 0006) |
| `PondRelease` | true | H2: a pond a player opens loses its water above the opening, which collects at the lowest point its flow reaches; the sea, rivers and large lakes take it without rising (decision 0007) |
| `Flatten` | true | H3 (sideways spreading on lower water; falling water in the air has no switch) |
| `Rapids` | true | H4 |
| `MaxDropScan` | 384 | H4: the deepest drop measured below an edge, in blocks; also bounds H3's and H4's walks along a column |
| `QueryRapids` | true | H4: where Earth's query marks rapids (query 3: the drops of a rapids reach), water is rapid and stays rapid |
| `QueryFlowLabels` | true | H4: river sources keep the flow label from Earth's query (query 2: rapids reaches; 3: every river column). Its own switch since 0.6.2 |
| `QueryWheels`, `WheelPowerRef`, `WheelMaxBoost` | true, 10, 3 | H8: a wheel over natural river water takes more torque from slope × discharge |
| `RapidSourceFoam`, `RapidFlowingFoam`, `CalmSourceSpecks` | 0.2, 0.5, 0 | C1 (client): the share of particle ticks that spawn foam on rapid sources, flowing rapid water and calm labelled sources (1: vanilla) |
| `ChunkPackFix` | auto | the engine workaround for lost block edits: auto (game 1.22.x), on, off |

The settling rules (H7: `Survival`, `FallLabel`, `NoSideFeedFromFalling`, `WakeOnFallLabel`, `LevelRelax`), H4's
`RapidSourceSheets`, `WakeEdgesOnNewSource` and `LoneVertical`, and `QueryNoVessels` (ignored where Earth's query answers)
are described in `src/HydrologyConfig.cs`. An unreadable `hydrology.json` is left as it is (the log says so) and the
defaults are used. H1 and H5 always apply. Bucket transport is vanilla's `noLiquidSourceTransport` world setting; Hydrology does not set it.

In the world settings' **Earth** tab, **Water flooding** (`hydrologyFlooding`, boolean, default true) enables or disables
the communicating-vessels part of H2: deep canals and basins fill toward their feeding water level. It overrides both
legacy vessel switches above when the key exists; `Fill` remains H2's master switch, and the depth, containment and
natural-river protections still apply. Ordinary ground filling, refill, rapids, settling and buckets keep their own rules.
The option can be changed after creation (`onlyDuringWorldCreate: false`): the server reads it at every fill, so it
applies at once; clients learn the value on joining. An Earth world whose steps need the river query keeps the vessels
case off while the query is not bound, whatever the option says.

No key is added to old saves by Hydrology. Without it, `FillVessels || (WorldMayFillVessels && EarthSavedFlag)` still
decides, with Earth's query-specific flag requiring its active river query as before. Mod-config values are never
overwritten by a world's choice. This build previously had no network config sync, so 0.1.13 adds a small settings
channel: modded clients request and receive the server's effective flooding value on joining. Since 0.6.1 Hydrology is
required on clients too (a client without it cannot join), and a client turns Hydrology off on a server without it.
Client foam settings retain their local configuration.

`/hydrology stats` (server admins) shows what the rules did since the world started.

## Building and testing

- `dotnet build` (Debug) builds `bin/Debug/mod`; `dotnet build -c Release` also writes `release/hydrology-<version>.zip`
  from a clean list of files (the DLL and its PDB, modinfo, worldconfig, LICENSE and the lang files) and refuses to
  overwrite an existing one.
- Server tests: [tests/server](tests/server) holds the `wateraudit` helper mod, `mkrun.py` and `analyze.py`. The options
  are listed at the top of `mkrun.py`. Earth worlds take the newest Earth and Cylinder World zips in the player's Mods
  folder, an Earth play style (`PLAY_STYLE`, default `earth`) and the player's `ModData/earth` store (its heights linked,
  not copied). Remove a run with `mkrun.py --remove <run dir>`. Example: [the caves check](docs/server-test-2026-11-13-caves-under-rivers.md).
- Offline regressions for issue #20, world flooding settings and enclosed pockets: `./tests/offline/run.ps1` compiles
  production H2 without game DLLs ([scope and baseline comparison](tests/offline/README.md)).
- The game-patch registry: `dotnet run --project tests/registry` (after a Debug build) patches the installed game's own
  DLLs in a test process: every target of H5, H8 and C1 resolves and is patched, the chunk-pack fix's targets resolve,
  and missing, changed or refused targets switch off only their feature, logged once, rolled back.
