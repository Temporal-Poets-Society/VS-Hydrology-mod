What Earth should generate

Earth 0.16.5 generates ordinary sources at a lip and ordinary falling water in the curtain, which turn rapid at the first update (seen at Niagara). Its step already stays as generated. If Earth draws its drops as below, nothing changes on the first update or after any of 1,796 pokes, under the recommended rules and under 0.1.3 (labels aside):

  • curtain lip: every source beside the curtain, both layers of the 2-deep lip, as rapidwater-<dir>-7;
  • curtain: rapidwater-d-6 up to the upper water's level, top cell included;
  • curtain bottom cell, on the plunge pool: rapidwater-<dir>-6; the ramp 5 to 1 after it as now;
  • chute step edge: the upper segment's top source as rapidwater-<dir>-7;
  • chute spill: rapidwater-d-3 above the first ramp cell of the next segment;
  • 1-block step edge: water-<dir>-7 (label only).

Remaining risks

  • 3.7% of undone edit sequences still end in a different state. Under 0.1.3 it was 13.7%. Most leave a ramp one height off. Some sources are made on a floor a player put in and then took away, which moves a step by one cell. A dead-end channel beside a ramp stays dry, because vanilla sends water only toward a drop.
  • Every result here is from the simulator, not the game. The port follows the decompiled 1.22.7 behaviour and reproduces the known in-game results, but the server test harness has to confirm it.
  • Later targets (ponds, diversions, dam failure, locks, long runs, power) were played with crude prototype rules: none is blocked by the recommended set (see the report). Long runs need new liquid blocks for sub-levels.
  • The scenes run along one axis. Diagonal rivers, frozen water, water wheels as blocks and chunk edges were not modelled. Pool layout 4's lake rim is modelled from its description, not from Earth's code.
